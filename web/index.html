HR Compliance Workflow

Initiating an assessment routes the case through the multi-agent VERITAS engine:

  • Classification Agent: Analyzes employment structure against statutory definitions.
  • Statutory Citation Agent: Evaluates Section 4 provisos and continuous service under Section 2A.
  • Dual-Route Guardrail: Automatically routes straightforward claims to answer-with-citation and ambiguous provisos to legal-counsel-review.
  • No Auto-Approval: VERITAS requires human HR review with mandatory justification before finalizing claims.
Audit Trail Enforced: All initiated assessments, AI runs, and subsequent review decisions are permanently committed to the immutable compliance audit log.
