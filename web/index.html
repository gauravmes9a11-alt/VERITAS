Statutory Framework & Reasoning Architecture

The desk operates on a strict verification principle: No conclusion is legally valid if it cites a primary statutory rule without actively searching for and verifying modifying provisos.

CoSS 2020 s.53(1)
Primary Rule: 5 Years
Gratuity payable to an employee on termination after continuous service of not less than five years.
CoSS 2020 s.53(1) Proviso 2
Modifying Proviso: Fixed Term
5-year requirement shall not be necessary on expiry of fixed-term contract; payable on pro-rata basis.
PoSH Act 2013 s.4
Internal Complaints Committee
Every employer of an establishment employing 10 or more workers shall constitute an Internal Committee.
