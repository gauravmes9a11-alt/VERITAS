1. Commencement of the Labour Codes.
The Central Government appoints the twenty-first day of November, 2025 as the date on which the Code on Wages, the Industrial Relations Code, the Code on Social Security and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code come into force.

2. Repeal of enactments.
On commencement, the enactments consolidated by the Codes are repealed, including the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, subject to the savings and transitional provisions of each Code.
